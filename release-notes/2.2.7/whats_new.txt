Latest changes:
- Enter incident name and briefing notes on the tablet. Record the connected map automatically, reuse details across batteries, and clear them for a new incident.
- Track mapped DJI video without RID; initialize AOL from its explicit altitude-reference coordinate.
- Prepare offline 1 m AOL tiles in Download Map using USGS lidar. Reuse complete coverage within Maximum Tile Age; expanded regions may need preparation.
- Share prepared AOL tiles through Mutual Aid packages, preserving age.
- AOL means Above Obstacle Level: aircraft elevation minus the highest mapped surface within a 200 ft horizontal radius. AOL -2 ft means that surface is 2 ft higher than the aircraft, not necessarily directly below it. Prepared tiles are required; wires or unmapped obstacles may be missing.
- Show cancellable USGS preparation and retries; restart map inactivity grace after preparation.
- Show POS? for stale telemetry, Unk for unavailable data, and -- for pending work. AOL stays Unk if its reference is missing. Only negative AOL turns red. Retain numeric AOL up to 1.5 seconds during refresh, including DJI reference updates; stale or unavailable results apply immediately.
- Open drone confirmation from a live stream that uniquely matches a configured drone designator, without repeating confirmation when RID arrives. Stream confirmation does not establish a launch reference.
- To calibrate ATO: hover at an independently established 50 ft directly above launch. In MapPane, tap the drone to focus it, tap it again for settings, then select "Calibrate at 50 ft over launch". Fresh telemetry is required.
- Edit aircraft with Save/Cancel, keeping local equipment and weights. Validate the aircraft and refresh edit authorization when saving.
- Confirm each flight; unanswered DCPs do not publish or archive. Remember aircraft equipment and payload; show equipment names and clearer pilot warnings; stable iPad menu. Keep flight type per organization and briefings per flight. Avoid duplicate Apple uploads.
- Retain readiness and operating-profile history, match RPIC callsigns to members, and show the verified organization user beside the rules controls. Tracker v1.4.90 preserves older configurations and improves RPIC, local-time, and readiness reporting.
- Import Tracker SVG enrollment QR files. Resume protected configuration downloads after sign-in.
- Follow a newly arriving single stream, including delayed RID telemetry. Tap once to focus and again for settings; panning releases focus. Ignore lasts for the app session.
- Prepare terrain around the device's one-mile operating area. Download smaller S1M pieces with detail and overlapping edges. Use terrain for straight-down clue projection even without heading or AGL.
- Automatically manage a shared map/terrain/icon/elevation cache. Default to 10 GB or 80% of free storage, preserving saved limits.

Platform-specific changes:
- Android avoids a cache-maintenance lock wait before the first screen, stabilizes map status-label width, and corrects render-rate rounding while retaining adaptive video pacing.
- Both platforms use designator/time/date track titles, also in standalone mode. Standalone flights stay independent, with confirmation and archive uploads.
- Apple uses compact markers in narrow map panes, bounds enrollment waits, logs import timing, and cleans the cache automatically. Map cache size and age use labeled keyboard-entry panels.

Known platform differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup/transfer; Google Drive is selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose Wi-Fi RSSI and may suspend video/anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded. Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering remains unavailable on iOS.
