Latest changes:
- Kept camera controls clear of the split-pane resize touch area.
- Drones kept unpublished remain quiet on later RID-only flights; a new local video stream offers confirmation again without automatically enabling publication.
- Tap the Bridge signal chip to switch between Main Screen and Live View, including full screen. This replaces title-bar swipe navigation; Settings can silence bridge audio warnings for the current app session; warnings are enabled again at the next app launch.
- The Video Streams update interval now controls both thumbnails and LiveTrack position updates, from 0.5 to 60 seconds with a 5-second default.
- Live View refreshes the network name and controller RTMP addresses when connections change, including switching between Wi-Fi networks.
- Clue reports include the captured drone location, altitude, designator, position source, and available flight and camera telemetry alongside the projected clue location.
- Improved Main Screen layout on narrow screens and with larger text: status controls wrap below the title instead of squeezing the title and Teams label.
- Improved spacing around system navigation bars and screen cutouts, including Settings, Live View, and the startup acknowledgement.
- Prevented stale drone confirmation prompts after a flight ends, including unanswered panels reappearing later.
- Fly standalone, then choose an incident map for each saved flight and its associated publishable clues. Flights kept local remain local.
- Improved recovery when incident-map connectivity drops, is slow, or only some requests succeed: pending track updates and uploads remain available for retry.
- Interrupted track uploads can resume after an app restart or update.
- Completed flight uploads are no longer repeated because cleanup failed, preventing old flights from reappearing after deletion.
- Airspace, NOTAM, and land-restriction checks refresh when network connectivity returns.
