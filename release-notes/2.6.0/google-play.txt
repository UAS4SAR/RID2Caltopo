Turning off Capture Streams now asks first, and a notice on the map shows when a live stream is not being recorded, with one tap to start recording. If the SD card becomes read-only or fails, live video keeps running, recordings and logs are kept on the tablet and copied to the archive later, and you get one alert instead of repeated pop-ups. Restored or imported settings no longer turn stream capture off. Location is used only while the app is visible.
