Latest changes:
- LiveTrack sends only the newest position, with at least five seconds between completed updates and the next transmission, avoiding a burst of old positions after an outage.
- LiveTrack starts retain the same identity through timeouts and retries to avoid duplicate tracks.
- Recorded waypoints share a one-second cadence and are retained for the complete finalized track, independently of live-update throttling.
- Incident-map reads refresh incrementally about every 20 seconds; a full reload remains available on request.
- Flight confirmation resets for the next flight without reopening for a completed flight, and unanswered flights remain excluded from recording and publishing.
- Improved live-video buffering and presentation, reduced redundant screen updates, and faster rendering of long map tracks.
- Video headers remain compact and stable; detailed delay measurements stay in diagnostics.
- Proximity alerts are optional and disabled by default. Enabling them explains telemetry accuracy and reporting limitations.
- Proximity spacing defaults to 100 feet and can be set as low as 50 feet, with allowances for reported position uncertainty.
- Standalone proximity alerts work for locally confirmed flights without requiring an incident map or Tracker ownership lease.
- Choose Published only for alerts involving your claimed aircraft, or All aircraft for any received pair.
- Stale and future-dated positions are excluded from proximity checks; warnings clear even when no new packets arrive.
- Incompatible or uncertain altitude readings retain horizontal proximity warnings and show vertical separation as unknown.
- Proximity status clearly shows Off, On, or Suspended, with accessible details, resume and disable controls, and warnings available in Live View.
- Aircraft markers use accepted reported positions. Predictive heads and assumed-motion proximity padding have been removed.
- Improved return from device lock, retaining the current screen and avoiding an unnecessary second unlock prompt.
- Main-screen coordination status is labeled Tracker.
- Updated operational acknowledgement with versioned local acceptance, read-only access from the main menu, and offline software license and notices.
- Optional video-telemetry research capture has bounded sample and text limits and preserves original recordings for diagnosis; it remains off by default.
- Reduced routine logging, including duplicate relay messages and per-position details, while retaining failure, recovery and track-finalization diagnostics.
