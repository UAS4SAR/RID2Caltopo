Latest changes:
- Long offline-map downloads no longer trigger automatic closing when no Remote ID messages are present.
- Download Map now shows current and projected storage accurately, accepts direct cache-limit entry, confirms saved limits, and moves automatically to visible progress and cancellation status.
- Map-download progress is weighted by downloaded bytes instead of tile count, so the final percentage better reflects the work remaining.
- Cache cleanup now runs separately from tile writes and waits for an active offline-map download to finish, preventing limit enforcement from stalling normal downloads as the cache fills.
- Apple keeps downloaded map tiles in durable device storage, refreshes expired tiles before reporting readiness, coordinates cleanup with live map loading, and retries transient terrain-download failures.
- Incident maps initially show Markers, Range Rings, Lines & Polygons, and App Tracks consistently on Android and Apple.
- Stream direction and clue reports now use fresh camera azimuth when available, including local magnetic-declination correction to true north for more accurate clue placement.

Platform-specific changes:
- Android keeps offline-map tile workers alive across screen lock and reauthentication, protects active downloads while the display sleeps, and recovers stalled network requests.
- Android accepts the secure OS unlock that immediately follows screen-off instead of asking for the device PIN a second time.
- Android and Apple both keep an active map download protected and begin a fresh idle interval after it finishes or is cancelled.
- Android and Apple use the same pre-download storage checks and editable cache-limit controls.
- Android removes its former local defaults that hid four built-in CalTopo map groups; server visibility and operator choices are still honored.

Known platform differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records it as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS and remains an outstanding parity gap.
