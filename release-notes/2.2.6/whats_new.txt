Latest changes:
- Drone icons fade to 50% opacity after five seconds without a new position and 25% after ten seconds, while a POS? indicator distinguishes stale position data from continued Remote ID reception.
- The Pilot Callsign entered in Drone Confirmation now controls the track label, stream-tile heading, and map label; the confirmation defaults from the Main Screen callsign while the RTMP routing designator remains stable.
- Visible incident maps refresh CalTopo artifacts incrementally, including folder and assignment renames, moves, deletions, and completed drone-track archival, without reloading the entire map.
- Clue snapshots prefer complete DJI SEI telemetry aligned to the rendered video frame, including camera azimuth, aircraft position, relative altitude, and the home-location terrain reference; RID remains the fallback when SEI is unavailable.
- Stream direction is labeled CAM when camera azimuth is available and TRK when only aircraft track is available, avoiding a misleading generic heading label.

Platform-specific changes:
- Android same-tablet reauthentication asks using the tablet's existing Tracker name and preserves that name when confirmed, without presenting a provisional replacement name.
- Apple selects the controller-facing Wi-Fi address from the active network path, with wired Ethernet as fallback, and omits the default RTMP port from the displayed endpoint.
- Apple correctly decodes multi-byte offline GeoTIFF elevation rows used for terrain-aware clue projection.

Known platform differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records it as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS and remains an outstanding parity gap.
