    Overlap and advanced settings

    Nadir (straight down), single grid. Ground resolution and height are a flat-ground camera estimate; terrain elevation can change the true ground detail and clearance. Height is relative to takeoff. Review actual route parameters and terrain clearance in Pilot 2 before flight. Presets are starting points, subject to a test flight.

    Direct route delivery to Pilot 2 and automatic photo return are not available in this build. This optional local station is a development fallback; the intended workflow configures, transfers and reconstructs on this R2C device.

    Manual boundary export for testing

    Download the boundary KML and move it to the controller using a transfer method supported by your device. In Pilot 2, import the boundary into the mapping route planner, then review the camera, route, height, speed, overlap and terrain clearance before flight. The template is experimental and requires separate qualification; Pilot 2 must generate and review the route before flight.
