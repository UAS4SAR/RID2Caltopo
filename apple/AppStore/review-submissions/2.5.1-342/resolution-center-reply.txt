Thank you for reviewing RID2Caltopo. Our answers to your questions are below.

1. Does the app connect with specific designated hardware brands? If yes, what are the brands?

RID2Caltopo implements industry-standard protocols for its core hardware interoperability: passive monitoring of supported ASTM F3411 Remote ID broadcasts over Bluetooth, and reception of RTMP video streams through its integrated MediaMTX server. These functions are not restricted to a single hardware manufacturer.

We have verified interoperability with DJI and Autel Robotics controller video, and BlueMark Innovations / DroneScout DS110 Remote ID relays. These are tested examples; compatibility depends on supported protocols, formats and configuration, rather than the brand name alone.

2. Are you authorized to distribute this app on behalf of these brands? If yes, please provide authorized documentation.

We are not distributing RID2Caltopo on behalf of these manufacturers. RID2Caltopo is independently developed and distributed under its own name. Manufacturer and product names identify tested interoperability; they do not imply sponsorship, endorsement or that we are acting as an authorized manufacturer representative.
